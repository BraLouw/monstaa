READY, MONSTAA?

Run, jump, collect coins and grab power-ups. The first boss appears after five hazards; every boss you beat adds five more hazards before the next one. When the boss arrives, Monstaa lands, walks up close, then punches!

HOW TO PLAY
SPACE / ↑ = jump · X = punch · P = pause
Collect coins, hearts and shields. Survive each level!

Sound effects are made in-game. Click once to start audio. Works on keyboard and touch screens.
